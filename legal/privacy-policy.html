Privacy Policy

Self Forged

Effective date: October 7, 2026

Self Forged is provided by Caleb Clark. Contact: calebkimballclark@gmail.com.

What this app stores

The app stores your diet logs, weight entries, workouts, habits, streaks, and related settings on this device only. We do not create accounts, and we do not operate a backend that receives your tracking data.

What we do not collect

We do not collect your name, email, location, contacts, photos, payment info, or HealthKit data. We do not sell data. We do not use advertising or analytics SDKs. We do not use your information for tracking across other companies’ apps or websites.

App updates

If you install a version from the App Store that checks for over-the-air updates, the app may contact Expo’s update service to see whether a newer version is available. That request can include technical details such as IP address, device type, OS version, and app version. It is used only to deliver app updates. Your diet, gym, and habit entries are not included in those requests.

Clipboard

If you use Import or Export, the app may copy or paste text you choose. It does not read the clipboard in the background.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices

You can export a copy of your local data or delete all local app data from Settings. Uninstalling the app also removes local data from that device. Because data is not stored on our servers, we cannot recover it after it is deleted.

Not medical advice

This app is a personal tracking tool. It is not medical care, diagnosis, treatment, or professional advice. Consult a qualified professional before starting a diet or exercise program.
